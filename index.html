Heritage & hospitality

Tea has always
been about more
than the cup.

Originally steeped by two brothers seeking stillness, Qahveh was born from a desire for quiet healing. This warm herbal spice tea pairs mild heat with grounding botanicals to foster a deeply relaxing atmosphere. Rooted in timeless contemplation, it is a restorative presence meant to be slowly and quietly experienced.

The result is something familiar yet distinctly its own: a tea meant not simply to be consumed, but experienced.
